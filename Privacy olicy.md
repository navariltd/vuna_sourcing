
**Privacy Policy**

**Navari Limited** ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed and safeguarded when you use our services. These include our website [Navari Limited](https://navari.co.ke/), our application **Vuna Sourcing** (a supplier and customer self-service portal for ERPNext), and any other related services that link to this Privacy Policy.

Vuna Sourcing runs on the ERPNext site of the business that installs it (the "Site Owner"). Information entered in the portal is stored on that site and controlled by the Site Owner. Navari Limited does not receive a copy of it.

**1. Information We Collect**

**Personal Information You Provide:** We collect the information you enter when you use the portal or contact us. This includes:

-   **Account Details:** your name, email address and password, used to sign in to the portal.
-   **Business Details:** when you apply to become a supplier or customer, the business name, logo, country, tax registration number, company registration number, website, billing currency and payment terms you provide.
-   **Contacts and Addresses:** names, email addresses, phone numbers and the business, billing and delivery addresses you add.
-   **Bank Details:** bank name, account number, account holder name, IBAN, branch code and SWIFT/BIC, used so the Site Owner can pay suppliers.
-   **Supporting Documents:** files you upload with an application, such as registration certificates or tax compliance documents. These are stored as private files on the Site Owner's site.
-   **Transaction Information:** the quotations, bids, orders and credit note requests you submit, and the documents the Site Owner shares with you, such as requests for quotation, purchase orders, sales orders, delivery notes, invoices and account statements.

**Automatically Collected Information:** We don't collect information about your device or how you use our services for analytics or advertising. The portal keeps a few preferences in your browser, such as the account and branch you last used, a list or grid view choice, and recently loaded portal data, so pages open faster. This information stays on your device. The site's standard session cookie keeps you signed in.

**2. How We Use Your Information**

The information is processed to:

-   **Provide and Manage Services:** let you apply as a supplier or customer, respond to requests for quotation, take part in bids, place orders, follow invoices and statements, and request credit notes.
-   **Review Applications and Transactions:** let the Site Owner's staff review onboarding applications, evaluate and award quotations, and approve or reject credit note requests.
-   **Communicate With You:** send emails about your application, award decisions and credit note requests, using the Site Owner's email account.
-   **Keep the Portal Secure:** make sure each user sees only the documents of the suppliers, customers and branches they are linked to.

**3. Information Sharing**

Your information may be shared:

-   **With the Site Owner:** the business you deal with through the portal, and its authorized staff, for the purposes above.
-   **With Other Bidders, Limited to Open Bids:** in a live auction, other invited suppliers can see bid amounts and rankings while bidding is open. Sealed bids stay hidden from everyone, including the Site Owner's staff, until the opening committee opens them.
-   **With Third-Party Service Providers:** for services such as server hosting and maintenance. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and may not use your information for any purpose other than providing services.
-   **In Case of Business Transfers:** if Navari Limited is involved in a merger, sale or acquisition, information we hold may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell your information, and Vuna Sourcing does not send your information to any outside service.

**4. Data Retention**

Your information is kept for as long as needed for the purposes in this Privacy Policy, unless the law requires or permits a longer period. Transaction records such as orders and invoices may need to be kept for the period accounting and tax laws require. When the retention period ends, your data will be deleted or anonymized.

**5. Security of Your Information**

We implement organizational and technical measures to protect your data:

-   Portal access is limited by role.
-   Each user only sees documents linked to their own supplier or customer accounts, and branches where assigned.
-   Uploaded documents are stored as private files.
-   Sealed bids stay hidden until they are opened.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

**6. Minors**

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we discover that we have collected such data by mistake, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

**7. Your Privacy Rights**

You can review and update your profile, contacts and addresses through the portal. To delete your account or business information, contact the Site Owner, who controls the data on their site. Site Owners can remove the data held by Vuna Sourcing by uninstalling the app from their site.

**8. Do-Not-Track Features**

We do not currently respond to Do-Not-Track signals or any other mechanism that allows consumers to opt out of tracking on the web.
